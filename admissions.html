ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Academic qualifications required for admission to TITI programmes.

1. Ugandan Applicants

Diploma Programmes

  • Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) with at least ONE PRINCIPAL PASS AND ONE SUBSIDIARY PASS, or an equivalent recognised qualification.
  • Applicants may also qualify through other recognised admission routes provided under the applicable regulations.

Certificate Programmes

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE), or an equivalent recognised qualification.
  • Applicants must meet any additional entry requirements specified for their chosen certificate programme.

2. Kenyan Applicants

Diploma Programmes

  • Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) with a minimum mean grade of C- (MINUS), or an equivalent recognised qualification.
  • A relevant Craft or Certificate qualification may also be considered where accepted under the applicable examination and programme requirements.

Certificate Programmes

  • Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) with a minimum mean grade of D (PLAIN), or an equivalent recognised qualification.

3. Applicants from Other Countries

Applicants from countries other than Uganda and Kenya are welcome to apply. They must submit their academic qualifications and supporting documents to the Institute for assessment.

  • Academic certificates obtained from their respective countries.
  • Academic transcripts or examination result slips.
  • Other relevant educational documents requested by the Institute.

The Institute will assess the submitted qualifications to establish their equivalence to the relevant Ugandan academic qualifications and determine the applicant's eligibility for the chosen programme.

Applicants with foreign qualifications must also satisfy applicable qualification recognition and English-language proficiency requirements.

IMPORTANT ADMISSION INFORMATION

Meeting the general entry requirements does not automatically guarantee admission. All applicants must satisfy the applicable programme-specific requirements and undergo verification of their academic documents.

For foreign qualifications, equivalence to UCE or UACE is determined by UNEB, while equivalence of higher education qualifications is determined by NCHE, as applicable.
