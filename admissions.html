ADMISSIONS INFORMATION | 01

ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Academic qualifications required for admission to TITI programmes.

Diploma Programmes – Kenyan Applicants

  • KCSE mean grade of at least C- (minus), or an equivalent qualification.

Certificate Programmes – Kenyan Applicants

  • KCSE mean grade of at least D+ (plus).

Applicants from Uganda and Other Countries

Applicants from Uganda and other countries should present their academic qualifications for assessment against the relevant programme entry requirements.

Applicants are encouraged to confirm the specific entry requirements for their chosen programme with the Institute before submitting an application.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

Prepare the following documents when making your application.

Application Documents

  • Academic certificates.
  • Academic transcripts or result slips.
  • Recent passport-size photograph.
  • National identification card or passport.

Important Document Guidance

Upload clear and readable copies of the required documents through the online application form.

Ensure that the information and documents submitted are accurate and complete to support the assessment of your application.
