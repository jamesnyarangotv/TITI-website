ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Applicants should meet the entry requirements applicable to their chosen programme.

Certificate Programmes

  • Relevant academic qualification from a recognised institution.
  • Academic documents showing previous studies.
  • Any additional requirements specified for the programme.

Diploma Programmes

  • Relevant certificate or other recognised entry qualification.
  • Academic transcripts or certificates.
  • Any additional programme-specific requirements.
Important: Entry requirements may vary by programme. Applicants should confirm the specific requirements with the Institute before applying.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

Prepare the following documents when making your application.

Academic certificates and transcripts
National identification document or other valid identification
Recent passport-size photographs
Completed application form
Relevant professional or supporting certificates, where applicable
Any additional documents requested by the Institute

FEES & PAYMENT

Understand your financial obligations before commencing your studies.

Tuition Fees and Other Charges

Fees depend on the programme and level of study. Applicants should obtain the current official fee structure from the Institute before making payments.

Students should follow the official payment instructions provided by the Institute and retain payment receipts for reference.

Payment Notice: Always confirm the official payment details with the Institute before transferring any money. Do not rely on unverified payment instructions.
