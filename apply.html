01

Admission Requirements

Applicants should meet the relevant academic entry requirements for the programme they intend to pursue.

Diploma Programmes – Kenyan Applicants

KCSE mean grade of at least C- (minus), or an equivalent qualification.

Certificate Programmes – Kenyan Applicants

KCSE mean grade of at least D (plain), or an equivalent qualification.

Diploma Programmes – Ugandan Applicants

At least one Principal Pass in the Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE), or an equivalent qualification.

Certificate Programmes – Ugandan Applicants

Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE), or an equivalent qualification.

Applicants from Other Countries: Applicants from other countries should present their academic documents for assessment against the relevant programme entry requirements.
02

Required Documents

Prepare the following documents when applying for admission:

  • Academic certificates
  • Academic transcripts or result slips
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • National identification card or passport

Upload clear and readable copies through the online application form. The application form indicates which documents are compulsory.

03

Intakes & Admission Periods

Regular Certificate and Diploma programmes have three intakes every year:

JANUARY Regular Intake
MAY Regular Intake
AUGUST Regular Intake
SHORT COURSES – ENROL ANY TIME!

Short courses and professional training are available throughout the year. You do not need to wait for the regular intakes.
