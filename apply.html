WELCOME TO TAHIDI INTERNATIONAL TRAINING INSTITUTE

We welcome prospective students seeking quality education, professional training and practical skills. Explore our admission requirements, intakes and payment information before completing your online application.

01. ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Diploma Programmes – Kenyan Applicants

  • KCSE mean grade of at least C- (minus), or an equivalent qualification.

Certificate Programmes – Kenyan Applicants

  • KCSE mean grade of at least D+ (plus).
Applicants from Uganda and other countries should present their academic qualifications for assessment against the relevant programme entry requirements.
02. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

Prepare the following documents when applying:

  • Academic certificates.
  • Academic transcripts or result slips.
  • Recent passport-size photograph.
  • National identification card or passport.

Upload clear and readable copies through the online application form below.

03. INTAKES & ADMISSION

Regular Certificate and Diploma programmes have three intakes every year:

JANUARY Intake
MAY Intake
AUGUST Intake
SHORT COURSES – ENROL ANY TIME!

Short courses and professional training are available throughout the year. Open to anyone interested in acquiring new skills and knowledge. You do not need to wait for the regular intakes.
