OUR INSTITUTION

Building Knowledge. Developing Skills. Creating Opportunities.

Tahidi International Training Institute (TITI) is a training institution based in Busia, Uganda. The Institute provides educational and professional training opportunities for individuals seeking to acquire knowledge, develop practical abilities and improve their prospects in the world of work.

We recognise that education must go beyond the acquisition of theoretical knowledge. Learners also need practical competencies, professional discipline and the confidence to apply what they learn in real-life situations.

Our work is therefore centred on education, vocational and professional skills development, and the preparation of individuals for employment, self-employment and entrepreneurship.

We welcome learners with different ambitions and backgrounds, and seek to provide a learning environment in which knowledge, ability, responsibility and personal growth are valued.

At TITI, our purpose is to make learning meaningful by connecting knowledge with practical skills and opportunities for personal and professional development.

OUR VISION & MISSION

Our vision sets the direction of the Institute, while our mission explains the purpose behind our work.

OUR VISION

Where We Aspire to Be

To become a leading centre of excellence in education, skills development and professional training, empowering individuals to achieve their full potential and contribute meaningfully to society.

OUR MISSION

What Drives Our Purpose

To provide quality, accessible and practical education through innovative teaching, professional development and skills-based training that prepares learners for employment, entrepreneurship and responsible citizenship.

OUR CORE VALUES

These values guide how we conduct our work, relate with our learners and fulfil our responsibilities as an educational institution.

01

Excellence

We strive for high standards in teaching, learning, student support and the delivery of our institutional services.

02

Integrity

We value honesty, fairness and transparency in our decisions, relationships and day-to-day responsibilities.

03

Professionalism

We encourage competence, discipline, responsibility and respect in the learning environment and workplace.

04

Innovation

We encourage new ideas, creativity and the use of relevant approaches to improve teaching, learning and skills development.

05

Accountability

We take responsibility for our actions, decisions and the duties entrusted to us by our learners and community.

06

Respect

We treat every individual with dignity and fairness, recognising the importance of equal opportunity and positive relationships.

OUR COMMITMENT TO STUDENTS

Choosing an institution for further education and training is an important decision. At Tahidi International Training Institute, we recognise the responsibility that comes with preparing learners for their future.

We are committed to providing a learning environment that encourages participation, supports the development of practical abilities and helps learners build the knowledge and confidence needed in their chosen fields.

We also recognise the importance of discipline, good conduct, continuous learning and the ability to adapt to changing workplace and business demands.

Our interest goes beyond the completion of a course. We want every learner to leave with knowledge and skills that can be put to meaningful use.
